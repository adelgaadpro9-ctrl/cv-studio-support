Privacy Policy

CV Studio · Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version

CV Studio has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your CVs, photos and settings stay on your device, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on iCloud sync. We, the makers of CV Studio, never receive them.

What stays on your device

Everything you type or import, your photos, your country editions and your settings are saved only on this device. Importing a scan, a PDF, a Word file or a LinkedIn export is read on the device. Writing help, speech-to-text, translation, text recognition and the ATS X-ray all run on the device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, your CVs, country editions and photos are stored in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account so they match on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy; only you can open it, and we cannot see or access it. Turn sync off at any time; copies already in iCloud stay there until you delete them in your device's iCloud settings.

Optional Claude features

If you choose to add your own Claude API key, the deep review and cover-letter tools send the text of your CV and any job description you paste to Anthropic, which runs Claude, to produce the answer. Your name, contact details, links, address, date of birth, other personal details and photo are never sent; a cover letter comes back signed with a placeholder that the app replaces with your name on the device. This happens only when you start one of those tools. Your key is kept in the device keychain and is used only to talk to Anthropic. Anthropic handles that data under its own commercial terms and privacy policy. You can remove the key at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and passes are sold and processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details. Apple tells the app only which purchase is active so it can unlock Pro.

Permissions

The camera is used only to scan a printed CV. The microphone and speech recognition are used only while you use Interview me, and your speech is turned into text on the device. You can turn each permission off in Settings.

Sharing

Files leave the device only when you export, share, print or send them yourself, and they go to the place you choose.

Children

CV Studio is meant for people preparing to apply for jobs. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a CV in the app, or delete the app, and its data is gone from the device. With iCloud sync on, deleting a CV also deletes it from iCloud and your other devices; to remove everything from iCloud, delete CV Studio's data in your device's iCloud storage settings. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on our website with a new date.
