Contact

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in CV Studio and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone, or the App Store › your account › Subscriptions on Mac. Job Hunt Passes don't renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
